GRAPH
----------------------------------------
Fastest graph algorithm overall: dijkstra_heap. Slowest: bellman_ford.
Dijkstra (heap) is typically fastest on sparse graphs. On dense graphs, Dijkstra (array) with O(V^2) becomes competitive because the heap overhead (log V per push) accumulates when E is large.
Bellman-Ford's early-exit optimisation causes actual iteration counts to be much less than V-1 on typical sparse graphs, partly explaining why its measured time is better than the O(V*E) bound suggests.
Dijkstra produces wrong answers on graphs with negative-weight edges. The dijkstra_negative_weight_failures.csv file lists vertex-level errors. This is not a bug - it is a known limitation of greedy relabelling.
SPFA matches or beats Bellman-Ford on most practical inputs because it only re-relaxes neighbours of vertices whose distance changed.
